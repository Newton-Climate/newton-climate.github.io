Service and Outreach

Cofounder: Systemic Access Mentorship Program

I cofounded this mentorship program to bring together blind students in STEM and give them tools for success. To date, there have been 40 matched mentors and mentees across 3 countries.

Cofounder, President, and Treasurer: Caltech Disability Coalition

To read more about this coalition, see this article from Caltech Magazine on creating a more inclusive Caltech.

Member: Caltech Graduate Student Council

I served as representative for the Engineering and Applied Sciences Division, and as a member of the Advocacy and Diversity Sub-Committee, from 2018 to 2020.

Member: Caltech Graduate Admissions Policy Committee

I was selected by the President of Caltech as student representative for the faculty committee tasked with increasing student body diversity and rewriting graduate admissions policies. This work involved extensive analysis of past admissions data and policies from other institutions, and resulted in a published white paper and a newly implemented admissions protocol. [Read the report]

Member: American Association of Physics Teachers Committee for Accessible Labs

As a member of this committee, I was tasked with making recommendations for university and K–12 physics lab accessibility. We published a white paper on this subject after thorough research on best practices for accessibility and education for disabled students. [Read the report]

Founder and President: Caltech Triathlon Club

In addition to assembling a team, coaching practices, and organizing the race logistics and schedule, I organized and coordinated a 3-race series in 2020 that involved 100 participants from 11 schools.

DEI committee work at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, DOE

While working at LBNL, I was a member of the lab-wide Employee Accessibility Committee, the Climate and Ecosystem Science Division representative for diversity and inclusion, and the DEI representative for the Department of Energy External Review Committee.
